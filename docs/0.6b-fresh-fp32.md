# Fresh FP32 LoRA epoch and observability

Experiment date: 2026-11-09. Requested after the
[LoRA literature experiments](0.6b-lora-literature.md).

## Registered experiment

Train rank-16 LoRA from the pinned original 0.6B base with freshly initialized
adapters and Adam state. Frozen base weights and autocast forward computation
remain BF16; adapter parameters, gradients and Adam moments are FP32. This is
mixed-precision LoRA, not a full model fine-tune or all-FP32 forward pass.

Use `configs/qwen06-lora.json`: all 28,001 retained clips, 1,750 updates with
16 clips per update and the final tiny remainder folded into the previous
update, peak LR 5e-5 with 50-update warmup and cosine decay to 1e-5. Keep rank,
alpha, dropout, codebook objective and conditioning mixture from the previous
recipe. Complete the requested epoch; track patience without stopping the epoch.
Preserve the lowest validation-loss checkpoint, including matching Adam/RNG.
The unmodified base is also eligible for selection.

Compare the selected checkpoint against `fp32-continuation-v4` using all 44
unseen-speaker validation prompts, fixed references/decoding, pinned ASR, and
generation seeds **2026 and 2027**. Report each seed and pooled scores. Require
lower pooled WER without pooled CER regression for provisional promotion, and
report speaker-clustered uncertainty across seeds. Never select individual audio
retries. Keep test cohorts untouched. This is development selection, not an
independent test result or a claim about human-rated naturalness.

## Metrics and how to read them

- **Comparable loss probes:** all 44 unseen validation clips, one fixed clip
  from each of 91 familiar validation speakers, and one fixed training clip
  from each of those same 91 speakers, matched by target codec length. All use
  non-streaming ICL, evaluation mode and disabled dropout. Training references
  stay within training; validation references stay within validation.
- **Train–validation gaps:** familiar and unseen loss minus the fixed training
  probe. The familiar gap controls speaker identity and approximately duration;
  residual content differences remain. The unseen gap also includes speaker
  distribution shift. Neither gap alone proves overfitting.
- **Components and fairness:** first-codebook CE, residual-codebook CE,
  combined objective, token-weighted cohort means, equal-speaker means, and
  per-speaker/per-clip losses. Selection uses the mean of the familiar and
  unseen token-weighted objectives, excluding training probes.
- **Optimization health:** pre-clipping global/component gradient norms,
  fraction of updates clipped at norm 1, learning rates, and actual adapter
  movement between probes. Zero unchanged-fraction is not itself a quality goal.
- **Efficiency/coverage:** clips and codec frames seen, epoch fraction, step
  time, clips/frames per training second, peak allocated/reserved GPU memory,
  OOM retry count, and evaluation time. Training throughput excludes evaluation;
  elapsed loop time includes it.
- **Heuristic trend flags:** three-probe changes can flag possible overfitting,
  continued learning or a plateau. A plateau can reflect the learning rate,
  capacity, data or objective; it is not an automatic underfitting diagnosis.

Do not compare dropout-on mixed-conditioning minibatch loss directly with the
dropout-off validation curves. Use the fixed training probe for that comparison.
Frequent gradient clipping alone does not imply divergence: inspect the
pre-clipping norm, loss trend and actual parameter movement together. A small
gap does not prove the model has enough capacity or that pronunciation is good.
The dashboard lets you exclude initial steps, inspect point values, and download
CSV data. It refreshes every 60 seconds; the trainer regenerates it at each
100-update evaluation and at completion. JSON metrics are written atomically.

## Completed results

The run completed **all 28,001 clips / 1,750 updates**, with no OOM retries.
The best validation-loss checkpoint was the final update, 1,750. The training
loop timer reached 3,622 seconds before the final evaluation (preparation and
speech evaluation excluded); peak reserved GPU memory was **6.12 GiB**.
All 466 adapter tensors and Adam moments were verified FP32, selected weights
matched their optimizer checkpoint, and archived source hashes matched.

### Fit diagnostics

| Selected model | Matched training loss | Familiar validation | Unseen validation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Incumbent FP32 continuation | 3.34997 | 3.41274 | 3.43130 |
| Fresh FP32 epoch | **3.30862** | **3.37518** | **3.39855** |

The fresh run's familiar gap was 0.06657 (incumbent 0.06277), and unseen gap
0.08993 (incumbent 0.08133). Both training and validation continued improving
late in the epoch. There is **no clear classical overfitting divergence** in
these probes. This neither proves sufficient capacity nor establishes that
more training would improve generated speech. Gradients remained finite and
were frequently clipped; there was no detected numerical failure.

### Generated speech and promotion decision

Two fixed seeds, 44 prompts each, ten unseen speakers, 876 reference words and
5,660 reference characters in the pooled comparison:

| Generation seed | Incumbent CER | Fresh CER | Incumbent WER | Fresh WER |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2026 | 5.65% | 7.77% | 13.24% | 15.30% |
| 2027 | 5.62% | 5.27% | 14.61% | 13.47% |
| **Pooled** | **5.64%** | **6.52%** | **13.93%** | **14.38%** |

**Retain the incumbent `fp32-continuation-v4`.** The fresh candidate did not
meet the registered pooled WER/CER promotion criterion. The WER change was
+0.46 percentage points, with a speaker-clustered 95% bootstrap interval of
**[-4.20, +6.75] points**. The evidence does not establish a general superiority
of either recipe. The seed-dependent reversal is why both seeds are reported.

The fresh model improved WER on 32 recordings, tied on 28, and worsened on 28.
Recordings with WER at least 50% increased from 3 to 6. Case 020 at seed 2026
alone added 12 word errors and 57 character errors, exceeding the overall net
increase of 4 words / 50 characters across both seeds. Its generated duration
increased from 4.40 to 8.64 seconds and its ASR transcript repeated/garbled the
target. **It remains included in every pooled score.** These observations flag
generation robustness for review; ASR is not a human listening judgment.

### Resulting optimization direction

The next useful experiments should target free-generation robustness and
checkpoint selection, rather than extending training solely because codec
loss falls. Pre-register a small checkpoint shortlist or a lower learning-rate
comparison and score the same complete prompts/seeds. Keep the incumbent until
a candidate meets the full promotion criterion. Any decoding experiment must
be separate and apply the same settings to both models; do not retry and keep
only favorable recordings. More epochs, different ranks, and preference
training remain hypotheses, not validated improvements from this run.
