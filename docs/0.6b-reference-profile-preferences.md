# Reference-specific joint preference collection

The first direct-gradient profile reduced fitting CER and improved average
voice cosine, but its complete screen failed: supplied-reference CER 3.2710%
and cosine 0.980227629. Development remained unchanged at CER 2.0484% and cosine
0.985057265. The fitting pronunciation gain did not generalize to the reserved
texts or screening texts. The profile was not promoted.

The next experiment prepares multiple generated alternatives for new training
sentences. Unlike the previous codec-gradient direction, these comparisons can
explicitly prefer outcomes that improve pronunciation and voice together.
They will support a reference-specific preference objective; no preference
training update was executed in the collection phase. A separate registered
trainer passed all 50 baseline controls and completed all 16 updates with
complete audits at steps 0, 4, 8 and 16. No checkpoint met the joint target.
The driver finished normally at its maximum update count; no checkpoint was
promoted or entered full confirmation.

## Literature connection and limits

[Direct Preference Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) derives a
classification objective from paired preferences and a reference policy. Its
published experiments concern language models and human preferences, not this
Qwen TTS profile. Here the proposed labels come from fixed ASR error counts and
an internal speaker encoder. Applying that objective to a small personal voice
profile is an experiment, not a claimed reproduction or established improvement.
Previous shared-adapter preference experiments failed; the new distinction is
training for this reference with fresh texts and only its conditioning profile.

## Registered collection

`training/reference_profile_preference_collection.py` selects 16 fresh unique
texts from the original preference-training pool, using the existing deterministic
hash order. It excludes every target and reference transcript in the full
114-recording confirmation panel, and all 12 texts in the earlier profile
fitting/validation collection. The reference audio and its transcript provide
conditioning only. No evaluation output or target transcript enters training.

Each text receives four fixed draws, seeds 41301–41304, for 64 recordings.
Collection starts from the original zero-profile model, not the failed 0.5%
profile adjustment. Shared acoustic, attention and speaker-adapter weights
remain frozen. CPU threads are four and both TF32 options are disabled; the
talker and speaker scorer use CUDA FP32 with the existing CPU codec. Sampling
and ASR settings remain unchanged. Before collection, one known GPU baseline
waveform must reproduce exactly under the same reference and seed.

Generation, ASR scoring, independent speaker/provenance audit and pairing run
in separate processes. Every one of the 64 recordings must be present and
terminated before any training pairs can be used. Recordings are not dropped
to make a better-looking pool.

Within each text, a preferred output must have no more character errors and no
lower cosine than its alternative. There must also be at least one corrected
character, or a cosine gain of at least 0.001. Conflicting pronunciation/voice
tradeoffs do not form pairs. The existing `voice_preferences.joint_pairs` helper
enforces generated training-only origins and identical text/reference conditions.
All valid within-text pairs are retained; no evaluation-time best-of-N selection
is introduced.

Two focused tests passed for deterministic exclusion of old and evaluation
texts, unique selection, complete 64-condition coverage, rejection of reserved
or truncated records, and joint preference dominance. The collection and driver
are registered under the external `reference-profile-preferences` directory.
The native baseline control reproduced `profile-fit-000`, seed 41201, exactly
and verified unchanged weights. All 64 recordings then completed and passed the
independent audit. The collection produced 67 valid pairs across all 16 texts:
27 improve pronunciation and 40 improve voice at unchanged character error
count. Equal-text weighting assigns 41.5625% of objective mass to pronunciation
improvements and 58.4375% to voice-only improvements.

The pair winners have average CER 1.0543% and cosine 0.982151592 when counted as
pair endpoints. Only 8 of the 38 distinct winners meet both absolute thresholds;
23 of the 67 chosen endpoints do. Thus relative preference labels are not already
goal-quality targets. Chosen-minus-rejected duration averages +0.247 seconds,
with range -1.04 to +3.44 seconds. These statistics remain diagnostics, not new
filters applied after collection.

A read-only absolute-support audit finds joint-passing recordings for seven
of the 16 texts (eight recordings total). Cases 002, 003, 004, 007, 008, 010,
012, 014 and 015 have no joint pass among their four draws. Cases 003, 007,
010, 014 and 015 have no cosine above 0.984; case 002 has minimum sampled CER
4.396%. Cases 004, 008 and 012 meet individual thresholds in different draws,
but not jointly. Only 30.8333% of case-balanced preference mass has a chosen
endpoint that passes both absolute targets. These are coverage diagnostics,
not estimates of achievable quality ceilings. Strong fitting preference
accuracy alone cannot establish adequate capacity, adequate target support,
or generated-speech generalization.

## Registered preference-training pilot

Three agents separately implemented the trainer and checkpoint evaluator and
reviewed their correctness. The root integrated the changes and manages all
native model jobs sequentially. `reference_profile_preference_training.py` uses
case-balanced DPO with beta 0.1 and fixed zero-profile reference likelihoods.
It scores the full raw sequence, all 16 codebooks plus EOS, without per-token
normalization. Scalar derivatives from shared trajectories are accumulated so
each distinct trajectory needs only one backward replay per update.

Only the 1,024 FP32 profile parameters train. AdamW uses learning rate 0.0005,
zero weight decay and gradient clipping at 1.0. The extra speaker-input movement
is limited to 1% of the original vector norm. Checkpoints are 0, 4, 8 and 16;
the original model weights, fixed references and optimizer state are verified.
Three objective tests, three trainer tests and four evaluator tests passed.

Every checkpoint evaluates 50 recordings: all 16 fresh training texts at the
predeclared first seed, eight original reserved samples, and the unchanged
26-recording two-group CPU screen. Checkpoint zero must reproduce every baseline
waveform and score exactly. All later development waveforms must remain exact.
A joint screen pass stops for full confirmation. Otherwise two consecutive
checkpoints dominated by an earlier checkpoint in both custom CER and cosine
stop the pilot; a CER-only regression does not stop a simultaneous voice gain.

Observability separates pre-update fitting losses from post-update checkpoint
metrics and audited generated speech. It records preferred and rejected
likelihood changes separately, including the fraction where both decline;
preference accuracy alone can conceal that behavior. Input movement, gradients,
memory, duration/length correlations, fixed-probe acoustic probability mass,
training/reserved/screen CER and WER, original-reference cosine, per-clip changes
and all custom screening audio are retained outside Git.

Native reference caching has passed for all 64 training trajectories, including
tokenizer IDs, original reference vector/codes, termination and unchanged model
weights. The complete checkpoint-zero audit has now passed: all 50 baseline
waveforms and scores reproduce exactly, all recordings terminate, and all
original weights are unchanged. The first four updates completed with zero
likelihood replay error, finite gradients and unchanged original weights.
The post-update DPO loss is 0.653494, preference accuracy is 94.375%, and actual
speaker-input movement is 0.317181% of the original vector norm (limit 1%).
These fitting measurements did not translate to a joint speech improvement.

### Complete checkpoint-4 result

All 50 recordings completed, with no truncation and unchanged original weights.
All 20 inactive development waveforms reproduced exactly.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Development | 2.0484% | 2.0484% | 4.5977% | 4.5977% | 0.985057265 | 0.985057265 |
| Supplied reference | 1.6355% | 4.9065% | 4.6875% | 14.0625% | 0.979095777 | 0.979899585 |

| Training probes | 2.2290% | 2.6342% | 4.9689% | 4.3478% | 0.981831670 | 0.982676607 |

Observed pronunciation worsened while average internal cosine improved. The
training-probe CER also worsened, so this is not simply a conventional widening
training/validation CER gap. Improving pair-ranking loss is insufficient
evidence of better generated speech. The registered stopping rule continues
because no earlier checkpoint dominates step 4 in both custom metrics.

The supplemental 10,000-draw paired text-block bootstrap retains both seeds per
text. Custom CER change is +3.2710 percentage points (exploratory 95% interval
-1.5152 to +5.7895), and cosine change is +0.000803808 (interval -0.000821620
to +0.003433347). The small three-text screen and repeated selection limit
inference; these intervals neither change stopping nor certify a population
voice improvement. Reserved-sentence cosine change also has an interval
crossing zero. Audited per-record changes and all six custom audios are in the
external observability report.

### Final checkpoint-16 result and decision

The original driver process closed successfully with `maximum_updates_reached`.
All 50 recordings completed without truncation, with unchanged original
weights and exact inactive-development parity.

| Group | CER | WER | Original-reference cosine |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Development | 2.0484% | 4.5977% | 0.985057265 |
| Supplied reference | 3.9720% | 14.0625% | 0.981111487 |
| Reserved sentences | 8.7037% | 16.2791% | 0.982466087 |
| Training probes | 2.6342% | 7.4534% | 0.979805529 |

Custom CER improved slightly over step 8, but voice cosine reversed. Reserved
CER worsened substantially in this small panel, and training-probe cosine fell
again. The fitting DPO loss nevertheless decreased to 0.576447, with preference
accuracy 95.625%. The 1% input-displacement projection was active at updates
14, 15 and 16; likelihood replay error stayed zero and original weights stayed
frozen. No pair mass had both chosen and rejected likelihoods decreasing, and
the final log-ratio/length correlation was only 0.1642. Thus neither a numerical
failure nor that particular likelihood-displacement pattern explains the
generated-speech regression.

The finite pilot ended at its registered maximum, rather than a Pareto stop:
each checkpoint traded at least one custom metric against the others. The
result does not support blindly widening the movement limit. The next native
experiment is the separate, no-training generation-PAD ablation described in
`0.6b-next-reference-policy-probes.md`. Its driver was launched only after the
original DPO process closed; registration passed and historical control
generation began. No PAD quality result is available yet.

The separately reviewed `reference_profile_preference_confirmation.py` is now
ready for a passing DPO checkpoint. It requires a terminal pilot with a complete
50-recording audit and an actual strict 26-recording joint screen pass. The
original 114-recording panel, reference audio and extra case metadata remain
unchanged; all 26 screening waveforms must reproduce exactly. Four tests cover
entry thresholds, complete scope, metadata, waveform parity and rejection of
nonfinite independent speaker scores. No native confirmation has launched.

The audited checkpoint-zero development screen has CER 2.0484%, WER 4.5977%
and cosine 0.985057265. The supplied-reference screen has CER 1.6355%, WER
4.6875% and cosine 0.979095777. Fresh training probes have CER 2.2290%, WER
4.9689% and cosine 0.981831670; reserved sentences have CER 3.8889%, WER
10.4651% and cosine 0.980691284. These are the unchanged starting point.

The goal remains CER strictly below 3% and original-reference internal cosine
strictly above 0.984 in both development and supplied-reference groups, followed
by the complete registered 114-recording confirmation. No new quality result or
trained profile has passed these requirements.
