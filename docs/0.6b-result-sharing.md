# Result sharing preference

User instruction, 2026-11-11: “everytime you get a better result, send me an example”.

Whenever a verified candidate improves the pronunciation and reference-voice
tradeoff, send a playable local audio example proactively, together with its
text and a concise comparison against the previous best. Report group-level
CER and voice cosine separately from the example's individual scores. Identify
the evaluated panel and whether the result is provisional or confirmed on the
complete original panel. A favorable single clip does not establish a better
model. If one metric improves while the other worsens, describe that tradeoff
instead of claiming an unqualified new best.

Keep generated audio and evaluation artifacts outside Git. Preserve the
registered texts and seeds; do not select favorable generations to determine
model quality. Use the same comparison text when available for listening.

For masked-supervision v2, the external
`summarize-masked-supervision-improvement.py` reads the independently verified
80-recording observer and prepares sharing drafts. It uses
`masked-custom-calibration-000`, seed 2026, at every checkpoint, chosen by
registered order rather than the clip's score. A positive checkpoint becomes a
new calibration best if it crosses both groups' quality and termination
requirements when the previous best did not, or improves
at least one of the four group CER/cosine metrics without worsening another.
WER and any fixed-example regression are reported separately. Calibration
improvements remain provisional until original114 confirmation.
Step zero's exclusion from training qualification is not itself a quality
failure: a positive checkpoint with identical already-passing measurements
must not be announced as an improvement.

The helper never sends a notification or marks an example as sent. Root must
render the verified absolute audio path in the conversation. An optional
external ledger may record only examples actually sent, bound to the run,
checkpoint, audit and audio hashes; rerunning the helper must not fabricate
delivery or suppress an unsent example.
