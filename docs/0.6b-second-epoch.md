## Completed training

The additional pass completed all 1,750 updates and 28,001 clips, bringing the
fresh model to **3,500 cumulative updates / 56,002 clip exposures**. The selected
checkpoint is the final update, with selection loss **3.36690**, down from
3.38686 at the end of epoch 1. There were no OOM retries; peak reserved GPU
memory was 6.13 GiB. Training took about 65.6 minutes before final evaluation.

Matched, dropout-disabled teacher-forced probes:

| Training | 3.30862 | 3.28816 |
| Familiar-speaker validation | 3.37518 | 3.35227 |
| Unseen-speaker validation | 3.39855 | 3.38152 |
| Familiar validation minus training | 0.06657 | 0.06412 |

All three losses improved across the extra epoch. The familiar gap narrowed,
while the unseen gap widened slightly. These probes show no clear sustained
overfitting divergence, but they do not establish underfitting or predict
free-generation quality. The last few probes have mixed trends; use the
generated-speech comparison to decide whether to promote this checkpoint.

**Select `fresh-fp32-epoch2` as the provisional development winner.** Its final
checkpoint meets the registered criterion: lower pooled WER without a CER
regression. Relative to the incumbent, WER fell **2.63 percentage points
(18.9% relative)** and CER fell 0.85 points. Relative to epoch 1, WER fell
3.08 points and CER fell 1.73 points; both seeds improved.

The incumbent comparison remains seed-sensitive: epoch 2 regressed slightly
on seed 2026 and improved substantially on seed 2027. The pooled WER change's
speaker-clustered 95% bootstrap interval is **[-5.53, +1.34] percentage points**;
against epoch 1 it is [-8.94, +1.30]. These intervals include no improvement.
This is a provisional selection on a small, repeatedly used development set,
not a statistically established general advantage or a naturalness judgment.
Independent test cohorts and human listening ratings were not used.

Against the incumbent, WER improved on 35 recordings, tied on 33, and worsened
on 20. There were 23 fewer word errors and 48 fewer character errors. Recordings
with WER at least 50% remained at three, versus six for epoch 1. Large seed-2026
regressions remain: cases 010 and 041 each add six word errors, and case 002 adds
four. All outputs remain in every pooled score. The blind listening page
contains the incumbent and both fresh epochs with randomized labels.
