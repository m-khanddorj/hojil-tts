reproduction of the 26 historical screening outputs, finite independently

needs matching teacher-forced and autoregressive conditioning, separate
training/calibration examples, and evaluation of both groups.

## Half-PAD control verified

All 26 custom-reference control recordings completed and passed their native
audit and independent observer checks. Pooled CER is 4.315305%, WER 11.278195%,
and cosine 0.982505299, with unchanged weights, no truncation and zero independent
cosine discrepancy. Compared with this experiment's unrouted baseline, voice
similarity improves but character error increases. This is a tradeoff, not a
new best or a goal pass. The full-PAD candidate stage is now generating.

## Next intervention being prepared

The paired error diagnosis independently recomputed all 228 records from the
two completed full-panel policies. Development errors shift across sentences:
22 recordings improve and 29 worsen. Short texts also regress; a long-text-only
curriculum is not supported. Shared ASR substitutions do not establish their
phonetic cause.

A new supervised pronunciation adapter is being prepared in separate files.
It will use real paired same-speaker recordings and a shared adapter that can
change development outputs, with direct updates excluded from reference/control
rows. Teacher replay must match native inference, including separate prefix and
continuation PAD embeddings. Reference branches must retain input gradients.
Only new adapter parameters may train. Exact parity and frozen-weight checks
precede fitting; direct reference masking is not a guarantee of preserved voice.

Data provenance and fitting/calibration separation are being independently
checked. Cached half-precision speaker vectors cannot establish exact native
FP32 reference conditioning; their reuse as if freshly extracted is prohibited.
No new training has started, and no inspected evaluation target is fitting data.

## Final candidate verified

The original process completed successfully, and all three stages passed their
native audits and the independent observer. The final full-PAD candidate fails
the joint goal:

| Group | Recordings | Pooled CER | Pooled WER | Speaker-macro cosine |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Development | 88 | 4.399293% | 11.986301% | 0.984458801 |
| Supplied reference | 26 | 4.085155% | 11.278195% | 0.982749196 |

All 114 native conditioning/PAD traces verified; all 88 development outputs
exactly reproduce the baseline. All 26 custom prefills and first codec frames
match the half-PAD control. No recording truncated, all weights stayed fixed,
and the independently recomputed cosine discrepancy was zero. Candidate results
SHA-256 is `01132883192d4cb71b726540e28c81ac847d7f15407d173a91f75bca04a1519b`.

Changing recurrent PAD improves custom CER and cosine slightly against its
own control, but this candidate does not replace the earlier reference-preserving
result. That earlier centered-step-16/full-PAD policy has lower development CER
(4.222615%) and better custom CER/cosine (3.279632% / 0.983555874), while its
development cosine is slightly lower (0.984226523). Neither meets the goal.

The centered-step-16/full-PAD policy is the chosen frozen starting point for
the separate real-speech supervised pilot, because its remaining gaps to the
joint target are smaller. This is an experiment initialization decision, not
a global model promotion. The feature-only driver has now started; its 324
fresh assets will precede CUDA memory testing, native parity controls and any
optimizer updates.
